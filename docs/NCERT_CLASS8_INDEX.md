## Documented discrepancies

1. Curiosity's earlier portal range (`hecu1=0-12`) implied 12 chapters; the official Contents lists **13** — the record was corrected to 13 (`hecu1=0-13`).
2. Deepakam's earlier portal range (`hsde1=0-17`) implied 17 chapters; the official Contents lists **13 numbered chapters** plus three unnumbered grammar appendices (परिशिष्टम् 1–3) — the record was corrected to 13 (`hsde1=0-13`); appendices are not chapter slots.
3. Poorvi's earlier portal range (`hepr1=0-5`) counted **units**, not lessons; the Contents shows 5 units containing **15 untitled-numbered lessons** — the record was corrected to 15 (`hepr1=0-15`).
4. Grade-8 Mathematics is **two volumes**: Part-1 (hegp1, "Chapter 1 … Proportional Reasoning-1") and Part-II (hegp2, "Chapter 1 Fractions in Disguise … Chapter 7 Area"), seven chapters each. Part-II (first edition December 2025) was added to the catalogue as `ncert-8-ganita-prakash-2`.
5. CIET /audio-book/130 shows a single "Ganita Prakash, Class 8" page with seven generic chapter labels, so it cannot distinguish the two parts — the NCERT portal pages (`textbook.php?hegp1=`, `textbook.php?hegp2=`) are used as the reference for the two maths records instead.
