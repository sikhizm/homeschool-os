## Kaushal Vikas selection path

The official Contents distinguishes an essential foundation in each work-form
unit from a selected vocation path. The catalogue now represents this structure
explicitly rather than treating all twelve labels as one compulsory linear
sequence.

| Unit foundation in default path | Official select-one alternatives |
|---|---|
| Chapter 1 — Introduction to Agricultural Practices | Chapters 2–4: Rooftop Gardening; Precision Farming; Additional Vocations |
| Chapter 5 — Shaping Materials | Chapters 6–8: Construction; Apparel; Additional Vocations |
| Chapter 9 — Personal and Lifestyle Services | Chapters 10–12: Healthcare; Tourism; Additional Vocations |

The three foundations receive stable default session sets. The nine alternatives
remain browseable and show their official selection note in the chapter page.
They do **not** enter the automatic queue or receive a compulsory session URL.
A parent may record learner progress after using an alternative, but that record
is not a persisted family/school choice and does not create its session trio.
This is a scheduling boundary, not a claim about what a particular school must
choose.

## Learning engine boundary

There are **88 default-path Class 9 items**: 97 verified titles less nine
select-one alternatives. Each default item has the existing original session IDs:

```text
ncert:<chapterId>:read
ncert:<chapterId>:practice
ncert:<chapterId>:review
```

That is 264 original parent-led session tasks, plus existing grade activities.
They reuse the established progress, assessment and retention behavior; no
session IDs for earlier classes were regenerated. The prompts are not NCERT
text, official answers, curriculum outcomes or chapter-to-skill mappings.

## Contents boundaries retained

- **Khel Praveen:** 23 Contents chapters govern. The generic portal’s 30 entries
  are navigation, not a chapter count.
- **Kaveri:** associated poems/readings are not promoted to artificial chapters.
- **Ganita Manjari:** the two official parts are retained as separate volumes;
  the Part II generic portal range cannot override its six official Contents
  chapters.
- No Class 9 chapter has an automatic outcome mapping or learner-data write from
  browsing the catalogue.
